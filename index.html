About the data

Source
The Lahman Baseball Database (2025 release, seasons 1871–2025), maintained by SABR. The tables come from the R package Lahman version 14.0-0 on CRAN, which ships the same release. scripts/prepare_data.py downloads it and builds data/batting.csv; scripts/analysis.py computes every number on this page into data/findings.json.
What one row is
One player's batting totals for one team in one season (the Lahman Batting table). A player traded mid-season has one row per team. Player name, batting side, throwing arm and birth country come from the People table; team and franchise come from Teams.
Rows dropped
The source table has rows. This project keeps the American League and National League rows from 1901 to 2025. It drops rows from other leagues (19th-century leagues, the Federal League and the Negro Leagues), whose record-keeping is not comparable, and National League rows from before 1901, when the American League did not yet exist. Rows with zero at-bats (mostly pitchers who never batted) stay in the data; they add nothing to any total.
How rates are computed
Every rate is a ratio of totals over all rows in the group, not an average of player rates. Batting average = hits ÷ at-bats. Home runs per 100 at-bats = 100 × home runs ÷ at-bats. Strikeouts per 100 at-bats = 100 × strikeouts ÷ at-bats. Triples and home runs per 1,000 at-bats use 1,000 in place of 100. Era figures pool all seasons in the era. "HR rate vs. 1901" divides the 2025 home-run rate by the 1901 rate (the same for strikeouts).
Other definitions
Eras: Dead Ball 1901–1919, Live Ball 1920–1941, Integration 1942–1960, Expansion 1961–1976, Free Agency 1977–1993, Steroid 1994–2005, Modern 2006–2025. Regular hitters (Finding 5 only): rows with at least 300 at-bats, so pitchers batting do not skew the comparison; rows with no batting side are left out. Foreign-born (Finding 6): birth country is not "USA"; each player counts once per season even if he played for two teams, and 2 rows with no birth country are left out. Franchises (Finding 4): teams are grouped by Lahman's franchise ID and named after the team the franchise fielded in 2025.

Every number above can be checked on the dashboard, which works from the same data file.
